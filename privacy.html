Privacy Policy

This policy describes how LockIn handles your information when you use the app.

Data We Collect

We collect your university email address, name, school or department, course preferences, and usage patterns within the app. We do not collect financial information directly.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to match students with tutors, improve recommendations, and enhance the platform experience. We analyze usage patterns to improve features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your profile information is visible to other users at supported universities. Tutor names are visible to all users, including guests who browse without signing in. We may share anonymized data for research purposes.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. All data is stored on secure servers. We regularly audit our security practices.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can request a full export of your data through the Settings page.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session and analyze usage. You can opt out of usage tracking in Settings while still using all core features.

Children's Privacy

LockIn is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us immediately.

Contact Us

For privacy concerns, contact us at lockin.customersupport@gmail.com. We respond to all privacy inquiries within 5 business days.
