I’m having trouble signing in.

Use the university email address you signed up with and check that you’ve verified it. Look in your inbox and spam folder for the verification email. If you try to sign in before verifying, LockIn sends another link.

If you still can’t sign in or need help with your password, email support from the address associated with your account. Never send your password or verification codes.

How do I find and contact a tutor?

Open Explore → Tutors, choose your school or department, course, and instructor, then tap Contact on a tutor’s profile. You can browse as a guest; sign in and complete your profile to contact tutors and book sessions.

Where are my sessions, and how do I change one?

Open Social → Sessions for upcoming tutor bookings and saved study group plans. Open a tutor’s chat and tap their name or icon to view session details, change a date and time, or cancel. Past tutor sessions appear under History.

A requested session is pending until the tutor confirms it. Use the in-app chat to coordinate any changes.

How do study groups work?

Go to Explore → Groups to find a group, or choose Create to start your own. Save a group plan and choose a meeting date and time in its details to keep it in your schedule.

Changes to a saved group plan update your own schedule. Confirm the meeting details with the host.

Why isn’t my calendar event updating?

Add to Calendar saves a separate copy of the session to your phone’s calendar. If a session changes in LockIn, update or remove the old calendar event yourself.

If you can’t add an event, check Calendar access for LockIn in your phone’s Settings and make sure you have the latest version of the app.

Is LockIn free to use?

Browsing and creating an account are free. Tutors set their own prices, and any paid tutoring arrangement is made directly with the tutor. Confirm the price and session details with them before meeting.

How do I get help with my data or account?

Read our Privacy Policy online or in the app under Account → Settings. The Terms of Service are also available in the app. For privacy questions, account deletion, or help accessing or correcting your data, contact our support team from your account email and describe your request.
